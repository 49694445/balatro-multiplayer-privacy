Balatro Multiplayer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026. This policy applies to the Google Play Android closed-test release and its use of project-operated multiplayer services.

Request deletion of your data · Privacy contact

Who we are

Balatro Multiplayer is an unofficial multiplayer mod and launcher offered by Google Play developer daikewei. Privacy contact: 49694445@qq.com. We are independent of the creators and publisher of the original Balatro game.

Information we process

When you connect to multiplayer, the project-operated service receives your nickname, network IP address, connection and session identifiers, game and mod versions, room membership, gameplay actions, scores, bids and purchases made with in-game gold, and game-state information needed to run and recover a match. In-game gold is not payment-card or bank-account information. Service and diagnostic logs can contain these identifiers, technical events and error details.

Play Games sign-in and room visibility

Build 28 uses Google Play Games sign-in to authenticate access to the project-operated Hong Kong multiplayer service. You do not enter a separate game password. The app requests OpenID authorization, sends a one-time authorization code to our service over HTTPS, and the service exchanges it with Google and verifies the returned identity. It derives a stable game identifier and a play_ nickname from the Google subject identifier using SHA-256. We do not request your Google email address or personal profile details for this flow. Google passwords are not sent to our service.

The authorization code, Google tokens and original subject identifier are handled in memory in the project authentication code. A hash of the authorization code is held temporarily for replay protection. The derived game identity and its session associations are held in service memory; this flow does not create a password record in the ordinary account database. Local reconnect files are separated using a hash of the Play Games player identifier, and can include a game reconnect token and room or match state.

Google Play Games SDK 21.0.0 also handles platform sign-in and its own service information under Google’s privacy practices. Google documents gamer identity when creating or updating a Play Games profile and SDK analytics and diagnostics. This release does not use Google achievements, leaderboards, friends or cloud saves. These Google services are separate from our multiplayer recovery system.

Ownership of the original Google Play game remains a separate resource prerequisite. Room chat is disabled for the supported Google Play original-game resource connection. Your game nickname and permitted room and match information are visible to other participants. Do not put sensitive personal information in a nickname. Historical records from other versions may include chat messages.

Imported resources and local files

You supply your own legally obtained original game resources. The app accesses the selected resource file or the supported installed original-game package to import a local copy. Original game resources are not included in this app. Imported resources, settings, saves and local diagnostics are stored on your device. Multiplayer recovery sends supported game-state snapshots to the server; this is separate from importing original resources.

Diagnostics you choose to share

The app can prepare a diagnostic ZIP and open Android’s sharing interface. You decide whether to share it and select the receiving app and recipient; you may cancel. Generating the ZIP is separate from sending it to support. A report sent to us is used to investigate the reported problem. Server-side reconnect diagnostics are recorded automatically during multiplayer and are separate from this optional sharing action.

Why we process information

We use the information to connect players, display rooms and match status, synchronize gameplay, enforce match rules, restore supported sessions, diagnose errors and investigate abuse. We do not use the information described here for advertising.

Who receives information

Other room participants receive the permitted nickname, room and gameplay information described above. Google receives the one-time sign-in code from our service to authenticate the selected account. We do not send room state, game passwords or gameplay actions in that token exchange. Google also processes information through its Play Games SDK under its own privacy policy. Hosting, network and email providers may process information to operate the service and handle support. If you choose an external sharing app, its handling of your report is also subject to that provider’s privacy practices. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to investigate abuse and protect the service. Independently operated multiplayer servers have their own operators and practices; check those before connecting.

Location and connection security

The project-operated public multiplayer service is hosted in Hong Kong; connecting from elsewhere transfers service data there. Build 28 uses HTTPS with certificate verification for its normal Hong Kong game connection and the one-time sign-in code. The server also uses HTTPS to exchange the code with Google. Earlier releases and independently operated servers may have different transport behavior, including legacy unencrypted TCP. This description of build 28 is not a guarantee about every older version or third-party server.

Retention

Our retention limit for project-operated service logs and diagnostics received by support is 30 days. This limit also applies to identifiable copies and backups under our control. The existing reconnect-diagnostic cleanup runs at service startup and hourly and removes records once they reach 29 days; this specific control is not a claim that every type of copy is automatically cleaned. Active game-state recovery snapshots are cleared when the room ends or the recovery session expires. Minimal termination receipts may remain for up to 10 minutes to finish connection handling. Play sign-in session associations are normally reclaimed about 10 minutes after disconnecting when no active match remains; associations needed for match recovery remain until the relevant match is released. Replay-protection code hashes have a 10-minute validity window and expired entries are removed during subsequent exchanges. Local reconnect files can remain across account changes until the app cleans the relevant session or you remove local app data; no fixed automatic local retention period is claimed. Hosting and email providers may retain their own technical service records under their applicable policies; we do not represent those independently controlled records as covered by our automatic reconnect cleanup.

Requests and deletion

Email 49694445@qq.com to request access, correction or deletion of nickname, session, gameplay and diagnostic records we control. For Play Games sign-in, you may also request deletion of project-controlled records associated with your derived game identity. Include the approximate time, displayed game nickname and room code if known so we can locate the records. Do not send Google passwords, authorization codes, Google tokens or game reconnect tokens. We process requests within 30 days. We cannot delete copies already received and independently retained by other players or external services. This email request path is not an automatic permanent unlinking control. Signing in again with the same Google identity derives the same game identifier and starts a new service session. Deleting project-controlled records does not delete your Google account or Play Games profile. Google-held game data and profile settings can be managed separately through Google Play Games or Google Account settings. You may contact the same address about privacy rights applicable to you.

Removing local data

Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local imported resources, settings, saves and diagnostics. Back up saves you want to retain first. These actions do not themselves delete server-side records; use the support contact for those requests.

Audience and updates

This app targets adults aged 18 and over. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied personal information. We update this policy when our practices change and show the revision date above.
