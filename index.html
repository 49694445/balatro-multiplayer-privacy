Balatro Multiplayer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026. This policy applies to the Google Play Android closed-test release and its use of project-operated multiplayer services.

Who we are

Balatro Multiplayer is an unofficial multiplayer mod and launcher offered by Google Play developer daikewei. Privacy contact: 49694445@qq.com. We are independent of the creators and publisher of the original Balatro game.

Information we process

When you connect to multiplayer, the project-operated service receives your nickname, network IP address, connection and session identifiers, game and mod versions, room membership, gameplay actions, scores, bids and purchases made with in-game gold, and game-state information needed to run and recover a match. In-game gold is not payment-card or bank-account information. Service and diagnostic logs can contain these identifiers, technical events and error details.

Accounts and room visibility

The current closed-test flow does not require a registered account or Google account sign-in to this app. Ownership of the original Google Play game is a separate resource prerequisite. The closed-test configuration disables room chat. Your nickname and permitted room and match information are visible to other participants under the game visibility rules. Do not put passwords or sensitive personal information in your nickname. Historical records from other versions may include chat messages.

Imported resources and local files

You supply your own legally obtained original game resources. The app accesses the selected resource file or the supported installed original-game package to import a local copy. Original game resources are not included in this app. Imported resources, settings, saves and local diagnostics are stored on your device. Multiplayer recovery sends supported game-state snapshots to the server; this is separate from importing original resources.

Diagnostics you choose to share

The app can prepare a diagnostic ZIP and open Android’s sharing interface. You decide whether to share it and select the receiving app and recipient; you may cancel. Generating the ZIP is separate from sending it to support. A report sent to us is used to investigate the reported problem. Server-side reconnect diagnostics are recorded automatically during multiplayer and are separate from this optional sharing action.

Why we process information

We use the information to connect players, display rooms and match status, synchronize gameplay, enforce match rules, restore supported sessions, diagnose errors and investigate abuse. We do not use the information described here for advertising.

Who receives information

Other room participants receive the permitted nickname, room and gameplay information described above. Hosting, network and email providers may process information to operate the service and handle support. If you choose an external sharing app, its handling of your report is also subject to that provider’s privacy practices. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to investigate abuse and protect the service. Independently operated multiplayer servers have their own operators and practices; check those before connecting.

Location and connection security

The existing project-operated public multiplayer service is hosted in Hong Kong; connecting from elsewhere transfers service data there. Multiplayer game traffic currently uses an unencrypted TCP connection. HTTPS on this policy page does not encrypt the game connection. We do not claim that all data is encrypted in transit.

Retention

Our retention limit for project-operated service logs and diagnostics received by support is 30 days. This limit also applies to identifiable copies and backups under our control. The existing reconnect-diagnostic cleanup runs at service startup and hourly and removes records once they reach 29 days; this specific control is not a claim that every type of copy is automatically cleaned. Active game-state recovery snapshots are cleared when the room ends or the recovery session expires. Minimal termination receipts may remain for up to 10 minutes to finish connection handling. Hosting and email providers may retain their own technical service records under their applicable policies; we do not represent those independently controlled records as covered by our automatic reconnect cleanup.

Requests and deletion

Email 49694445@qq.com to request access, correction or deletion of identifiable records we control. Include the approximate time, nickname and room code if known so we can locate the relevant records. Do not send passwords or reconnect tokens. We process requests within 30 days. We cannot delete copies already received and independently retained by other players or external services. You may contact the same address about privacy rights applicable to you.

Removing local data

Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local imported resources, settings, saves and diagnostics. Back up saves you want to retain first. These actions do not themselves delete server-side records; use the support contact for those requests.

Audience and updates

This app targets adults aged 18 and over. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied personal information. We update this policy when our practices change and show the revision date above.
