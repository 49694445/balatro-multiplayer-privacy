Balatro Multiplayer — Privacy Policy

Review draft for the planned Google Play release — updated 24 November 2026. This draft is published for transparency and review; it is not yet the effective policy for that release. The proposed 30-day retention/deletion rules below require implementation and verification before activation. The final data inventory and service-provider review are still in progress.

Who we are

Balatro Multiplayer is an unofficial multiplayer mod and launcher, offered by Google Play developer daikewei. Privacy contact: 49694445@qq.com. This project is independent of the creators and publisher of the original Balatro game.

Information processed

When you connect to multiplayer, our service receives your chosen nickname, network IP address, connection/session identifiers, game and mod versions, room membership, gameplay actions, scores, in-game purchases and bids made with game gold, and the game-state information needed to run and recover a match. Game gold is not payment card or bank-account information. Server logs can contain nicknames, room codes, gameplay events, connection addresses, and chat messages.

Room chat is optional. Messages you send are delivered to other participants in your room and may be recorded in service logs. Your nickname, room information and supported match/status information are visible to other players according to the game's visibility rules. Do not put passwords or sensitive personal information in nicknames or messages.

The app stores imported game resources, settings, saves and diagnostic files on your device. Resource import accesses the file you select or the supported installed Balatro package needed for import; it is not intended to inventory unrelated installed apps. Game resources are not supplied by this project. Multiplayer recovery may send game-state snapshots to the server; this is separate from importing the original game resources.

Diagnostic logs may include error messages, version information, connection/recovery events and technical identifiers. The app can prepare a diagnostic archive and open Android's sharing interface. You decide whether to share that archive and which recipient/app to use. If you send it to support, we process it to investigate your request. Do not send unrelated personal files.

Why information is processed

We use these data to connect players, operate rooms and chat, enforce match rules, synchronize gameplay, recover supported sessions, troubleshoot errors, and protect the service against abuse. The reviewed multiplayer flow uses a nickname and session information rather than a registered user account.

Recipients and services

Other players receive the room/chat/game information described above. Infrastructure and support providers may process service data on our behalf to operate the service. Information you choose to send through an external sharing app is also subject to that app's privacy practices. We may disclose information where required by law or necessary to investigate abuse and protect the service. Independently operated multiplayer servers have their own operators and privacy practices; check those before connecting.

Server location and security

The current project-operated multiplayer service is hosted in Hong Kong, so connecting from another country may transfer data there. Current multiplayer TCP and HTTP update connections do not provide encryption for all data in transit. Do not send sensitive information through game chat. A future release that changes these practices will require an updated policy and store declaration.

Retention and deletion

For the planned release, service logs and diagnostic records received by support will be retained for no more than 30 days, once the proposed controls are implemented and verified. The planned release will clear active game-state recovery data when the room ends or its recovery session expires; limited termination/connection metadata may remain briefly to finish disconnection handling. Any copies or backups containing identifiable logs must follow the same maximum retention period.

You can request access, correction or deletion of identifiable nickname, chat or diagnostic records by emailing 49694445@qq.com. Include the approximate date/time, nickname and room code if known, so we can locate the relevant records; do not send passwords or reconnect tokens. The proposed release policy is to process the request within 30 days; the operational deletion procedure is being verified. You may also contact this address about rights available under applicable privacy law. We cannot remove copies other players or independent services have already received.

You can remove local imported resources, settings, saves and diagnostic files by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. These actions do not themselves delete server-side logs; contact us for server-side requests. Keep any local saves you wish to retain before clearing storage.

Audience and changes

This app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied personal information. We will update this policy when our practices change; the published page will state its effective date.
